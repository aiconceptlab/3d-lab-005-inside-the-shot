This manual takes itself apart. ☕

I turned a fictional espresso machine into a product manual you can talk to.

“Take it apart.”
The housing opens and the internal assemblies separate.

“How does the water move?”
An animated path connects the reservoir, pump, heater and brew group.

“Just show me the pump.”
The rest of the machine fades back.

“Remove the pump first.”
Blocked. The model shows which parts need to come off first.

Built with Higgsfield 3D Jutsu, Blender, Three.js and Anime.js. The free starter includes the interactive app, editable model, assembly rules and setup guide. Prepared commands work without API keys; a live language interpreter is optional.

This is a conceptual assembly with prepared internals and authored dependencies. It is not a scan of a real machine, a physics simulation or a repair manual.

Imagine this for product onboarding, training or interactive demonstrations.
